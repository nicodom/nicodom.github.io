- Built a Kafka-based AI product-enrichment pipeline in collaboration with the DS/AI team, with automated quality scoring and human review: 176,000+ products approved within three months of the May 2026 launch
- Took the AI Shopping Assistant from proof of concept to public launch (July 2026) through internal beta and dark deploys
- Supported cross-functional work between Engineering and the DS/AI team (separate departments) on a real-time recommendation engine built on Kafka, Flink and Iceberg on Confluent Cloud, and built a recommendations API that let the DS/AI team iterate independently of storefront engineering
- Stood up the MerchTools team (Oct 2023) and delivered the pricing platform: Scheduled Pricing (May 2024), Multi-Channel Pricing on its committed date (Oct 2024) and Athena, an AI-powered platform for real-time pricing and inventory optimization (2025); cut StoreManager API timeouts from 1 in 5 to 1 in 25
- Ran a continuous A/B testing programme on Eppo; wins included Save for Later ($5M annualised demand), Apple Pay on product pages and typo-tolerant search; also delivered the Afterpay UX redesign
- An Iterable-based marketing and member communications stack
- Drove org-wide adoption of AI-assisted development (Claude Code), shifting engineers from single-task to multi-task parallel workflows

- Security and compliance: PCI DSS v4 script monitoring (vendor selected over three alternatives, $25k/year), GDPR consent, passwordless login, and migration to DataDome bot protection on web, iOS and Android
