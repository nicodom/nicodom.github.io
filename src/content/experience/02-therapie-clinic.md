Hired as Director of Engineering, reporting to the CEO, with a clear mandate: build an internal tech organization from the ground up. When I joined, Thérapie Clinic had no internal engineering capability — all software was outsourced, managed by third-party consultancies, or replaced entirely by spreadsheets and manual processes. There was no engineering culture, no product ownership, and no strategic control over the technology underpinning a rapidly growing, multi-country business.

In two years, built a fully functioning tech organization comprising 7 teams and 45 people (~65–70% FE/BE/DevOps engineers and IT support, the rest Product Managers, Product Designers, and Data Analysts): 5 stream-aligned cross-functional teams, 1 platform team, and 1 IT support team.

**Key deliverables across the stream-aligned teams:**
- Built real-time data pipelines from scratch, introducing the "Data in Motion" paradigm — live streaming of online and offline customer behaviour into Twilio Segment (CDP) and downstream to Sendgrid, Looker, and Redshift
- Delivered full SAP ByD ERP integration in under a year, covering data ingestion, transformation, and synchronisation across multi-country clinic operations
- Built an in-house call centre platform integrated with Twilio Communication and Flex, capturing and routing communication data in real time
- Designed all software multi-tenant and multi-country from day one, supporting IE/UK operations and enabling full US market readiness in under 2 months
- Established a Datadog observability stack and LaunchDarkly feature management across all services and pipelines

Third-party integrations: Stripe, Twilio Communication, Flex, Segment, Sendgrid, LaunchDarkly, Datadog, Fivetran, Looker.
