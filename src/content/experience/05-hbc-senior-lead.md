Led a team of 5 software engineers, 1 product manager, 1 PMO, and 1 QA, owning the following sections of HBC's multi-banner environment (saksfifthavenue.com, saksoff5th.com, lordandtaylor.com, thebay.com/labaie.com): Mini-Cart, Cart, Checkout, and Order Submission.

The main project was moving shopping cart functionality for HBC's common platform out of the deprecated e-commerce monolith, creating a set of highly available, secure APIs to provide cart functionality for all sites and apps. The proposed solution used Event Sourcing (ES) and CQRS, backed by Kafka as an event log and Kafka Streams to fold the event stream into current state.

One of the most challenging parts of the project was designing the migration plan from the old to the new cart implementation without affecting customer experience. Also actively participated in hiring, internal mentoring, and training.
